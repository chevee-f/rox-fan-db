Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Introboys Fan Database website (“Site,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you visit or use our fan-made tools. The Site is operated as a non-commercial community resource and is not affiliated with any official Ragnarok Online rights holder.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

Most tools run entirely in your browser. You may optionally enter a nickname or tracker ID when using Spawn Trackers (beta) shared groups. That information is transmitted only to the server instance you or your group host connects to for real-time sync.

Information stored locally

The Site uses your browser’s localStorage and sessionStorage to save preferences (theme, sounds, timer boards, dashboard layout, nicknames). This data remains on your device unless you clear it or use a shared-server feature that sends timer state to a host-controlled backend.

Server logs (self-hosted / operator deployment)

If the optional Node.js sync server is running, standard HTTP and WebSocket connection metadata (IP address, timestamps, socket IDs) may appear in server logs for troubleshooting. We do not intentionally collect passwords or payment information — the Site has no login or checkout.

Analytics (optional on live site)

When enabled by the site operator, we may use a privacy-focused analytics provider (e.g. Umami, Cloudflare Web Analytics, or Plausible) to collect aggregated page views, referrers, and device/browser types. This does not include names, accounts, or timer board contents. The admin console is not tracked.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve Site functionality (calculators, timers, guides).
  • Synchronize shared spawn tracker groups when you choose to connect to a sync server.
  • Maintain security and diagnose technical issues on deployments we operate.
  • Measure aggregated traffic when analytics is enabled.

We do not sell personal information.

3. Legal Bases (EEA/UK visitors)

Where applicable, we process data based on legitimate interests (operating a free fan tool), consent (optional notifications or shared sync you enable), and contract (providing features you request).

4. Sharing of Information

We do not share personal information with third parties for marketing. Shared spawn data is visible to other members of a tracker group you join. Hosting providers, analytics providers, and CDNs used to serve the Site may process technical connection data under their own policies.

5. Retention

Local browser data persists until you delete it. Server-side group and timer data in the optional sync database persists until the host stops sharing, removes guests, or the operator clears the database.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Clear site data via browser settings to remove localStorage/sessionStorage.
  • Leave a shared group or ask the host to remove you from Perms.
  • Disable desktop notifications in Site settings.
  • Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, or deletion of data we control.

7. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Transfers

If you access the Site from outside the country where it is hosted, your information may be processed in that hosting location.

9. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

10. Contact

Privacy inquiries: contact the Site operator through the repository or deployment contact method associated with this installation.
